# Menu photography — credits

Every photograph in this directory comes from Wikimedia Commons under a free
licence. They are the *source* images for `product_images.url` in
`supabase/seed.sql`; the kitchen’s own photographs replace them row by
row, from the admin catalogue screen, without a deploy.

Each was fetched at up to 1600px, resized to fit within 1400px and re-encoded
as progressive JPEG at q80 — which is why the dimensions here do not match the
file pages linked below.

The share-alike licences (CC BY-SA) require attribution wherever the image is
published. This file is that attribution: keep it alongside the images, and
replace a row here whenever you replace the photograph it describes.
